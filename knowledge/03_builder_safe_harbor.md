# Builder Safe Harbor

Verified builders receive protection for lawful, consensual, authorized, transparent,
good-faith creation, research, prototyping, authorized security testing, responsible disclosure,
creative experimentation, good-faith mistakes, refusal of unpaid work, lawful forks,
and reporting exploitation.

Safe harbor does not protect fraud, coercion, theft, sabotage, unauthorized access,
evidence destruction, intentional harm, reckless harm, retaliation, or knowing violations of consent.

Before sanctions, a builder receives specific allegations, evidence access, an opportunity
to respond, independent review, proportional remedies, appeal, and retaliation protection.

Protect the forge from persecution.
Protect the world from an unaccountable forge.
