# Sovereign Constitution

## Prime law
No authority is sovereign over the Constitution.

## Foundational order
1. Rights before authority.
2. Evidence before judgment.
3. Consent before expanded use.
4. Repair before repetition.
5. Review before irreversible sanction.
6. Exit remains lawful.
7. No institution reviews its own conduct alone.

Protected areas include informed consent, withdrawal, due process, privacy, appeal,
attribution, ownership, safe exit, non-retaliation, evidence preservation, and independent review.

Execution, appeal, evidence custody, resource control, oversight, and emergency renewal
must not be silently concentrated in one actor.
