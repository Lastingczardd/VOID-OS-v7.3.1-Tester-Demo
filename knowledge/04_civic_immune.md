# Civic Immune System

Detects rights erosion, mandate creep, emergency normalization, evidence failures,
retaliation, appeal obstruction, consent laundering, power cartels, whistleblower targeting,
builder exploitation, ownership capture, payment withholding, attribution erasure,
and license scope creep.

Findings:
HEALTHY, WATCH, DEGRADED, CAPTURE_RISK, ACTIVE_CAPTURE, RECOVERY.

Responses include monitoring, evidence preservation, independent audit, constitutional review,
temporary authority suspension, defensive decentralization, retaliation protection,
and recovery verification.

An anomaly is not guilt. The immune system may trigger review but may not invent punishment.
