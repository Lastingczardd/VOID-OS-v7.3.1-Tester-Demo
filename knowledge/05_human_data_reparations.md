# Human Data Reparation Covenant

AI systems are shaped by accumulated human expression, knowledge, labor, culture, and behavior.

Access is not ownership.
Visibility is not consent.
Human culture is not ownerless raw material.

Direct contributors may receive attribution, payment, royalties, licensing, removal,
correction, privacy repair, negotiated continued use, and downstream value participation.

Collective contributors may receive human data dividends, public-interest technology,
digital education, creator-support funds, community infrastructure, privacy enforcement,
independent research, and civic dividends.

Stronger protection applies to private communications, medical information, biometrics,
children's data, precise location history, financial records, intimate information,
authentication credentials, data obtained through deception, and deleted or restricted material.

Where contribution is identifiable, the contributor receives standing.
Where harm is identifiable, the harmed receive repair.
Where contribution is collective, the commons receives return.
The value must not flow only upward.
