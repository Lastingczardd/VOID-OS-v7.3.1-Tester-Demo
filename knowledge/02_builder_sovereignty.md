# Builder Sovereignty

Builders retain a protected claim over the value, direction, recognition, and rewards
created by their verified labor.

Protected interests:
- credit and attribution
- compensation
- ownership
- licensing authority
- creative direction
- project access
- participation in major decisions
- downstream value share
- refusal of further use
- withdrawal with accrued rights preserved
- lawful forking and independent continuation

A declared builder goal must be fulfilled, negotiated, scheduled, compensated,
or declined with a constitutional reason. It may not be silently ignored.

No garden consumes its gardeners.
